Fengqi Group has advanced key development milestones at the Pahang Digital Halal Food Industrial Park (HALAL Industria Hub), its flagship cross-border industrial platform in Malaysia's Pahang state.

The 100-acre park in Gambang is designed to provide a one-stop platform for businesses targeting the ASEAN, Middle East and global halal markets.

The project leverages Malaysia's JAKIM halal certification system, which is recognised in 80+ countries. Applications typically take 6–12 months. With Fengqi's application support, the estimated timeline is 3–6 months.

The regional logistics network includes the East Coast Rail Link (ECRL) and the Kuantan Port–Port Klang direct shipping route. This combination can reduce cross-border customs clearance from approximately 72 hours to 48 hours for participating tenants.

Through RCEP and the Malaysia-EU Free Trade Agreement, businesses operating from the park benefit from tariff rates of 0–5% across key export markets.

Fengqi serves as overall industrial planner, resource integrator and operational coordinator for the park — providing tenants with end-to-end support from certification and logistics through to international market channel development.
